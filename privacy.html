MPI Field Tools
Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

MPI Field Tools is a restricted business application used by authorized Michigan Property Inspections personnel and approved subcontractors to coordinate field work, safety, communication, equipment, training, and management records.

Information we process

Depending on a user’s role and the features they use, the app may process:

  • name, company email address, phone number, profile photograph, role, inspector identifier, and approved work or end location;
  • scheduled job details, addresses, services, workflow actions, timestamps, hours worked, drive time, work status, training, equipment, and acknowledgment records;
  • precise device location during an active workday, including route points and verified arrival or Clock Off locations;
  • private team messages, read receipts, company updates, requests, safety notices, replies, and attachments;
  • photographs the user chooses to capture or attach, including inspection-support, Chain of Custody, equipment, safety, profile, or Comment Builder photographs; and
  • technical diagnostics needed to identify failed synchronization, notification, authentication, or Comment Builder requests.

How information is used

Information is used only for legitimate MPI business operations: assigning and completing work, verifying field activity, calculating effective time, supporting payroll review, communicating with the office, documenting safety and equipment, managing training, diagnosing app problems, and maintaining auditable company records.

Location information

When an authorized field user completes Morning Ready, the installed iPhone app may record workday location in the background. This supports live field status, job arrival verification, travel records, route history, and final Clock Off verification. Workday tracking stops when the user completes Clock Off. iOS location permission can be reviewed in the phone’s Settings app.

Photos and Comment Builder

The app accesses only photographs that a user captures or selects. Photos and written notes submitted to the MPI Comment Builder may be transmitted securely to MPI’s configured Google/Firebase AI service to generate inspection wording. The generated comment must be reviewed by the inspector. The app does not use this content for advertising.

Service providers

MPI uses service providers needed to operate the app, including Apple for app distribution and notifications, Google/Firebase for authentication, hosted operational records, notifications, and Comment Builder processing, and approved scheduling or company systems used in read-only workflows. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy commitments.

Sharing and tracking

MPI does not sell personal information, does not serve third-party advertising, and does not use app information to track users across other companies’ apps or websites. Information is shared only with authorized MPI personnel, approved service providers, or where legally required.

Retention and security

Operational records are retained for as long as needed for company operations, safety, payroll, training, equipment accountability, audit, and legal obligations. Access is role controlled. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections, and signed or corrected records preserve their audit history.

Access, correction, and deletion

Authorized users can ask MPI management to review or correct their profile and operational information. Requests to access or delete eligible information can be submitted using the contact information below. Certain payroll, safety, signed acknowledgment, or audit records may need to be retained.

Policy changes

MPI may update this policy when the app, company procedures, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
